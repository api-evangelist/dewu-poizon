# Dewu (POIZON)

> Dewu (POIZON) is a leading Chinese online marketplace for sneakers, streetwear, luxury goods, and collectibles, known for its integrated authentication (legit-check) service. It operates the POIZON Open Platform, a developer program that lets sellers, ISVs, and ERP partners integrate with the marketplace over an app-key + signature authenticated API.

## Developer

- [POIZON Open Platform (English)](https://open.poizon.com/): developer portal, API documentation, and sign tooling
- [Dewu Open Platform (得物开放平台, Chinese)](https://open.dewu.com/): domestic developer portal
- [API Introduction](https://open.poizon.com/doc/list/documentationDetail/15): overview of the Open Platform API
- [Seller Authentication](https://open.poizon.com/doc/list/documentationDetail/9): how sellers authenticate and authorize integrations

## Company

- [POIZON (global)](https://www.poizon.com/): global marketplace and legit-check app
- [Dewu (得物, China)](https://www.dewu.com/): domestic marketplace
